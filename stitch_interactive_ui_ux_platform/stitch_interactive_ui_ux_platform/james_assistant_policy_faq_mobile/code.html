What is the deadline and rebate structure for early property tax payment?

Property Tax • Municipal Citizen Charter & Regulations 2026 (p.12)

Property owners who pay their full annual property tax assessment on or before April 30 are entitled to an early-bird rebate of 10% on the general tax component. Payments made between May 1 and May 31 receive a reduced rebate of 5%.

VERBATIM OFFICIAL EXCERPT (CLAUSE)

“An early-bird rebate of 10% on general property tax shall be granted to property owners paying the entire annual dues by April 30, reducing to 5% through May 31.”
